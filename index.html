More Than Just a Game

The Snooker Central isn't just Noida's finest snooker destination — it's a sanctuary for those who appreciate the art of the game. Founded with a vision to create a space where sophistication meets sport, we've set the gold standard for premium entertainment.

Our handcrafted tables, world-class ambiance, and passionate community come together to create an experience that transcends the ordinary. Whether you're a seasoned pro or picking up a cue for the first time, this is where unforgettable moments are made.
